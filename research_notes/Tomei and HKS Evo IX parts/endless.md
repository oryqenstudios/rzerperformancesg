# ENDLESS (endless-sport.co.jp / Endless USA): product models for the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A)

Scope note: the Endless Japan site (www.endless-sport.co.jp) could not be reached from this research session; the network blocked it. The official data below comes from **Endless USA** (endlessusa.com: its CT9A system-kit and pad databases, product pages and spec pages). Japanese list prices and the current Japanese part codes come from **Blackhawk Japan**, a Japan-based exporter whose listings copy Endless's Japanese catalogue text. UK prices and some older codes come from **J-PN** (UK importer). Endless USA files every CT9A item under one vehicle: "Lancer Evo.VII/VIII/IX CT9A *Brembo CALIPER model (03-07)". So every part below is shared by the Evo VII, VIII and IX unless stated otherwise.

## Q1. Which Endless models exist for the CT9A Evo IX, with part numbers?

### Takeaway
Endless lists a full brake range for the Brembo-equipped CT9A (Evo VII/VIII/IX):
- **Monobloc calipers:** Racing MONO6, Racing MONO4, Racing MONO4r.
- **Lighter monobloc:** MONO6 Sports TA.
- **Two-piece calipers:** Racing6, 6POT, 6POT Compact Mini ("Chibi6"), M4, and the Racing4 and S2 rears.

Each caliper comes as a "brake kit" (keeps the factory 320×32 / 300×22 discs), a "system kit" (new stock-size discs) or a "system inch-up kit" (345, 355 or 370 mm front, 332 mm rear). Kits run from about US$2,200 to US$12,870 and from about ¥240k to ¥1.38M.

For the **factory Brembo calipers** there are pads (front **EP357**, rear **EP291**) in roughly 12 compounds, Racing and Basic/E-Slit discs (**ER611** front, **ER612** rear), Swivel brake lines (**EB603ST / EB603RC**) and brake fluid. Suspension is Endless's own coilover line (historically branded **ZEAL**; now sold as ENDLESS FUNCTION-PS/SC/TC/.COM, part codes **ZS603xx** / **ZS604xx**). No Endless clutch was found.

### Cited Findings

**A. Factory brake sizes and Endless part-number logic**
- The CT9A Brembo car's factory discs are listed as **320×32 front** and **300×22 rear**. Kits that keep them show "N/A (OEM 320x32)" / "N/A (OEM 300x22)" — [Endless USA kit 1502524](https://endlessusa.com/product/1502524)
- Kit types, as Endless's catalogue text defines them:
  - **Brake Kit:** no rotor; reuses the stock rotor.
  - **System kit:** a rotor the same size as stock.
  - **System inch-up kit:** a larger rotor.
  - "1PCS is genuine 1-piece type, 2PCS is 2-piece type, and 3PCS is rear 3-piece type. (Floating is a separate order)"
  - "The rotors for RacingMONO6/MONO6r/MONO4/MONO4r are E slit type, and all other rotors are carving slit type."
  - Source: [Blackhawk Japan EFY6XCT9A](https://blackhawkjapan.com/products/endless-efy6xct9a)
- Kits described "for vehicles equipped with genuine Brembo calipers" can also go on non-Brembo CT9A cars (e.g. Evo IX RS), except the brake kits (genuine-rotor kits). The brake line may differ, so the order must say so — [Blackhawk Japan ECY6BCT9A](https://blackhawkjapan.com/products/endless-ecy6bct9a)
- Code letters seen in Japanese codes:
  - suffixes: B = brake kit, S/T = system kit (1-piece / 2-piece rotor), X = system inch-up kit, Z = "system inch-up kit 2" (370 mm)
  - prefixes: EDY = Racing Mono, EFY = MONO6 Sports TA, EHY = Racing6, ECY = two-piece calipers
  - Sources: [Blackhawk Japan ECY6TCT9A](https://blackhawkjapan.com/products/endless-ecy6tct9a), [ECY6ZCT9A](https://blackhawkjapan.com/products/endless-ecy6zct9a), [EDYMXCT9A](https://blackhawkjapan.com/products/endless-edymxct9a)
- J-PN lists the same kits with a **Z** in third place (EDZ…, EFZ…, EHZ…, ECZ…). Examples: EDZMXCT9A (Racing Mono 6 system kit, front), EFZ6XCT9A (MONO6 Sports TA inch-up), ECZ6BCT9A (6 Piston brake kit) — [J-PN EDZMXCT9A](https://www.j-pn.co.uk/store/product/Endless-Racing-Mono-6-Piston-System-Kit-(Front)/EDZMXCT9A/), [J-PN EFZ6XCT9A](https://www.j-pn.co.uk/store/product/Endless-Mono-6-Piston-Sports-TA-Inch-Up-Brake-Kit-(Front)/EFZ6XCT9A/), [J-PN ECZ6BCT9A](https://www.j-pn.co.uk/store/product/x/ECZ6BCT9A/)
- J-PN lists fitment for all these kits as Evo VII GSR/RS, VIII GSR/RS, VIII MR, **IX GSR/GT/RS and IX MR GSR/RS** (CT9A, 4G63) — [J-PN EDZ4XCT9A](https://www.j-pn.co.uk/store/product/Endless-Racing-Mono-4-Piston-System-Kit-(Front)/EDZ4XCT9A/)

## Q3. Current vs discontinued?

### Takeaway
Every caliper kit, pad, disc and line above appears current in late 2026:
- Endless USA's CT9A kit list and caliper records were updated in August 2026, and Endless USA prices them.
