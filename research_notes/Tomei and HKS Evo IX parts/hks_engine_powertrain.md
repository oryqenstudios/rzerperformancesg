# HKS engine and powertrain parts for the Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A, 4G63 MIVEC turbo)

Scope: HKS (hks-power.co.jp) engine and powertrain product models that fit the Evo IX / IX MR (CT9A, 2005/03 to 2007/09), with notes where the same code also fits the Evo VII/VIII. Mufflers/cat-backs, suspension, brakes and aero are excluded (another researcher covers them).

How the data was gathered (read this before using the numbers):
- Almost everything below comes from HKS Japan's own product application tables. Each product series has an English listing filtered to Mitsubishi (`/en/product_search/product/{series}/maker/5`), and a Japanese copy of the same table that also shows the price (`/product_search/product/{series}/maker/5`). HKS also keeps a separate listing of the same series at `/status/9`. On every page checked, that listing held only rows marked "* Discontinued", so it is the discontinued list. Product detail pages (`/en/product_db/...` and the Japanese `/product_db/...`) give the photos and the tax-excluded price.
- **Prices:** the Japanese tables print each price as "¥X (¥Y)". ¥X is the retail price with 10% consumption tax and ¥Y is the price without tax. Both are given below. Prices were read on 2026-11-26.
- **Status:** "current" means the item is in HKS's current Mitsubishi table with no discontinued remark. "Make to order" (受注生産品) is HKS's own remark. "To be discontinued" (販売終了予定品) is also HKS's own remark.
- **hksusa.com** (HKS USA) could not be reached from this session's network, so no USD prices are included. The site would have to be allowed in the session's network settings to fetch it.
- **Photos** are HKS's copyright. The URLs below only record where the photos are; using them needs HKS's or a dealer's permission. Each image was downloaded once to check its size and what it shows.

## Which HKS models exist specifically for the CT9A Evo IX, with part numbers?

### Takeaway
HKS still lists a fairly complete CT9A engine programme for the Evo IX. It includes:
- the GTIII-RS Sports Turbine Kit (11004-AM007)
- an R-type intercooler (13001-AM005) and IC piping (13002-AM002)
- three intakes (Racing Suction 70020-AM103, Super Power Flow 70019-AM105, Premium Suction 70018-AM001)
- an SSQV4 blow-off kit (71008-AM011)
- a metal catalyzer (33005-AM002)
- an oil cooler (15004-AM110)
- the Coil Pro (43005-AM002) and a carbon plug cover (45999-AM002)
- 4G63 cams, head gaskets, forged pistons and rods
- a 2.3 L short block
- LA single and twin clutches (26010-AM001, 26011-AM001)

The GT3037S full kit, GT II 7460R KAI kit, stainless manifold, front pipe, S-type intercooler, injectors and fuel pump are discontinued. HKS lists no GT2835, GT3240, GTIII-SS or GT7460 (other than the GT II 7460R KAI) kit for the CT9A.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Turbochargers, turbo kits, outlet pipes

**GT III Sports Turbine Kit, turbo "GTIII-RS". Code 11004-AM007 (current)**
- **Fitment:**
  - HKS lists 11004-AM007 for Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A, 4G63, 05/03-06/08), IX MR (06/08-07/09) and VIII MR. Its turbine is "GTIII-RS" and its boost pressure is 98.0 kPa (1.0 kgf/cm2). — [HKS Sports Turbine Kit (Actuator series), Mitsubishi](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_search/product/476/maker/5)
  - Evo VII and VIII (not MR) use 11004-AM008, the same GTIII-RS at the same boost. — [same listing](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_search/product/476/maker/5); [VII detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20284)
- **What it is:** HKS calls the kit "for Lancer Evolution VIII MR-IX MR [CT9A]". The "GT III Turbine" combines an MHI CHRA (centre cartridge) with HKS's own vehicle-specific housings. Kit contents: GTIII-RS turbocharger assembly, gaskets, bolts etc. — [HKS detail page, Evo IX](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283)
- **Not for a stock fuel system:**
  - HKS says: "The 4G63 stock engine is able to achieve over 257kW(350ps); therefore, this kit is not designed to be installed to a stock fuel system vehicle specification."
  - An optional restrictor, 11014-AM001, holds boost to 98 kPa (1.0 kgf/cm2) or less.
  - A CHRA for overhaul is 11014-AK040.
  - Source: [HKS detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283)
  - The restrictor 11014-AM001 is marked "* Discontinued" in the GTIII option-parts table. — [HKS GTIII Turbine Option Parts (discontinued list)](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_search/product/782/status/9)
- **Fitting note:** the shape of the oil-return pipe mount depends on the engine's production lot. The fitter measures the clearance from the top of the oil pan (photos show 30 mm on Evo VII-VIII and 22 mm on Evo VIII MR-IX MR) to choose the right kit. — [HKS detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283)
- **Price:** ¥319,000 incl. tax (¥290,000 excl.), the same for 11004-AM007 and -AM008. — [HKS JP listing](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/product_search/product/476/maker/5); [HKS JP detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/product_db/turbo/db/20283)
- **Manual:** [11004-AM007.pdf](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/product_search/product/download/3894/ja_en/11004-AM007.pdf)
- **Not stated by HKS:** compressor/turbine wheel sizes and A/R (see Gaps).
- **Photo:**
  - `https://www.hks-power.co.jp/storage/syshks/product_db/W1rIyv3AIvMbLoXsas68KwSxQjwEMxW6OFmzYQm3.jpeg` (640×529): the whole kit on a dark grey studio gradient. Turbo with a bright machined compressor cover and a dark cast turbine housing, plus oil and water lines, gaskets and bolts.
  - The same page also has a "Special Restrictor" photo (`.../z8bPT2oy718NSNeyZLLIHJx1mcQsfiBcc0456bzj.jpeg`, a machined aluminium cone) and the oil-pan clearance diagram (`.../4MyQavDLQNvbuUGjCNJZ7WBDpxD51jMHJYKyUmyI.jpeg`).
  - Source: [HKS detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283)

### Inferences
- **Turbo kit for the configurator:** the GTIII-RS (11004-AM007) is the only HKS turbo kit for the CT9A still on HKS's list. GT3037S and GT II 7460R KAI should be listed as "legacy", or left out.
- **Evo IX cams (an inference from HKS's engine labels):**
  - The Evo IX has MIVEC on the intake cam. HKS labels 22002-AM011 "4G63 CT9A" (no MIVEC) and labels the MIVEC intake cam 22002-AM009, now discontinued. So for an Evo IX the current HKS cam is probably only the exhaust 22002-AM012, with 22001-AM001 springs.
  - The cam pulley is explicitly listed only up to the VIII MR.
  - The workshop should confirm this with an HKS dealer.
- **Combinations HKS forbids:**
  - The HKS R-type intercooler (13001-AM005) and the SSQV4 kit (71008-AM011/AM011V) cannot be combined. The SSQV4 also cannot be used with the HKS IC piping kit.
  - The configurator should treat "HKS intercooler + piping" and "HKS SSQV4" as mutually exclusive, or warn about it.
  - The intercooler also needs an HKS intake (Racing Suction or Super Power Flow).
- **F-CON iS:** with no Evo IX harness listed, an F-CON iS on a IX probably needs a dealer-made or older harness. HKS's own IX test car ran an F-CON iS (see the power-figures question below).

### Gaps
- **Turbo specs:** HKS does not publish the GTIII-RS's compressor/turbine wheel sizes, trim or A/R on the Evo pages, so they are not given here. The GT3037S kit's A/R (0.87) and turbine trim (56T) come from HKS's discontinued table.
- **Not checked:** HKS radiators, catch tanks and "Twin Power" ignition amplifiers. No series pages for them appeared in HKS's current English category indexes (cooling, electronics), so HKS probably does not offer them for the CT9A now. Not confirmed.
- **Missing codes and prices:**
  - the 4G63 2.3L kit (STEP1/STEP2); only the short block (23011-AM001) has a code and price
  - the Fuel Regulator 1407-RA015
  - the M35iL plug (no price captured)
- **Racing Suction "Reloaded":** whether 70020-AM103 / 70019-AM105 are the "Reloaded" generation is not stated.
- **Not seen directly:** HKS USA prices and part pages. hksusa.com could not be reached from this session; its URLs for 11004-AM007 / -AM008 and 70020-AM103 were seen only in search results.
- **Not done:** EvoM forum confirmation of fitment.

## What power figures does HKS quote for its 4G63 turbo kits?

### Takeaway
HKS's own dyno graph for the Evo IX GTIII-RS kit gives:
- **GTIII-RS:** 435 PS at 6,920 rpm and 53.5 kgf·m at 3,860 rpm, at 1.8 kgf/cm² boost
- **GT II 7460 (older kit):** 397 PS / 51.3 kgf·m at the same boost
- **GTIII-RS with the boost restrictor:** 310 PS / 35.5 kgf·m (about 1.0 kgf/cm²)

The test car had a stock 4G63 bottom end with fuel, exhaust, intercooler, intake and F-CON iS upgrades. For the 2.3L kit, HKS quotes a 500 PS bench result and a suggested limit of 500 PS / 60 kgf·m.

### Cited Findings
- **The dyno graph** on HKS's GTIII-RS Evo IX page (image `.../yx64COjMNvuR5noLONicfSGc8fOX04XU6KQ7L8QU.gif`) — [HKS detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283):
  - "GTIII-RS_1.8kg/cm² POWER 435PS/6920rpm 53.5kg·m/3860rpm"
  - "GTII 7460_1.8kg/cm² POWER 397PS/6840rpm 51.3kg·m/4030rpm"
  - "GTIII-RS with restrictor POWER 310PS/6880rpm 35.5kg·m/4960rpm" (the restrictor boost trace sits at about 1.0 kgf/cm²)
  - Test car on the graph: "4G63 E/G Normal, Fuel upgrade kit, Super Turbo Muffler, Metal Catalyzer, Intercooler Kit, Racing Suction, F-CON iS"
  - The page text lists the test vehicle as "Stock Engine; Large Capacity I/J, Large Capacity Fuel Pump; Super Turbo Muffler, Metal Catalyzer; Intercooler Kit; F-CON iS"
- **Listed boost:** HKS lists the kit at 98.0 kPa (1.0 kgf/cm2), while the 435 PS figure is at 1.8 kgf/cm². — [HKS listing](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_search/product/476/maker/5)
- **Stock engine potential:** "The 4G63 stock engine is able to achieve over 257kW(350ps)". This is why the kit is not meant for a stock fuel system. — [HKS detail page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_db/turbo/db/20283)
- **GT3037S (56T) A/R 0.87 full kit (discontinued):** HKS's table gives only its boost range, 107.9–137.3 kPa (1.1–1.4 kgf/cm2), with no power figure. — [HKS](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product_search/product/477/status/9)
- **4G63 2.3L kit:**
  - STEP1 in-house bench test: "Power: 500ps, RPM: 7000rpm"
  - STEP2: "Suggested power limit: 500ps Torque: 60kgm"
  - Source: [HKS 4G63 2.3L page](https://www.hks-power.co.jp/en/product/engine/capacity/4g63.html)

### Inferences
- The 435 PS figure is at the crank, from HKS's own test, at 1.8 kgf/cm² (about 176 kPa), well above the 1.0 kgf/cm² in the table. That boost needs HKS's fuel upgrades and an F-CON iS (or a reflash).
- For the configurator, "up to about 435 PS (HKS in-house, 1.8 kg/cm² boost, supporting mods)" is a fair label, with the 310 PS restrictor figure as the conservative setting.

### Gaps
- HKS gives no power figure for the GT II 7460R KAI at its listed 0.8 kgf/cm² boost, nor for the GT3037S full kit.
- Whether the graph is crank or wheel power is not stated. Japanese HKS graphs are usually engine/chassis-dyno "PS" figures; this is not confirmed.
