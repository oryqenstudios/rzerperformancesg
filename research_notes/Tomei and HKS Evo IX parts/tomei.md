## Which Tomei models exist for the CT9A Evo IX, with part numbers?

### Takeaway
Tomei's current range for the Evo IX is almost entirely engine-side: the EXPREME exhaust family (titanium cat-back, titanium cat straight pipe, stainless manifold, heat shield, outlet/front pipe), one ARMS turbo kit (M7963, P/N 173028), EVO9-specific PONCAM / PROCAM camshafts, valvetrain, metal head gaskets, 2.2/2.3 L stroker kits and internals, oil baffle, fuel pumps, an adjustable fuel-pressure regulator, a blow-off valve, a turbo suction hose, a timing belt and oil-filler caps. Tomei makes no radiator, oil cooler, intercooler, intake pod, ECU or body/aero parts for the Evo in its current database.

### Inferences
- For a configurator, the parts a customer would actually see on an Evo IX are few: the Expreme Ti cat-back (from behind the car), and in the engine bay the oil-filler cap, the turbo suction hose, the FPR, the heat protector and (partly) the ARMS turbo and BOV. Everything else (cams, springs, gaskets, internals, belt, baffle, pumps) is invisible once built and would only suit a "spec list" or job-card line, not a photo layer.
- The ARMS M7963 replaced the M7960 for the 4G63 (same Evo IV–IX fitment, M7960 manual dated 2016, M7963 page shows a spec table still labelled "ARMS M7960"); the US MX/BX7960/7967 kits seem to be older Tomei USA variants of the same family.
- Because the Evo IX uses MIVEC on the intake cam, Tomei sells EVO9-specific intake cams (1462…, 146A…) and sets (143058/143068); the Evo VII–VIII cams should not be offered for an IX.

### Gaps
- Tomei USA's own site (tomeiusa.com) could not be opened from this session, so US part numbers (TB/TA series) for cams, springs, the FPR, the BOV and the suction hose are unconfirmed; the Poncam US numbers seen in snippets (TA301F-MT01B for the Evo 9 EX cam, TA301D-MT01B intake, TA304A-MT01A springs) are retailer data only.
- No evidence was found of Tomei "M8280" or "MX8265" kits for the 4G63.
- No Tomei 22KIT catalogue page price was checked against the DB (the 23KIT page shows ¥598,000 vs ¥590,000 in the DB; the DB is likely the newer figure, but that is unconfirmed).

## What does the Expreme Ti exhaust for the Evo IX look like (tip count, size, finish, layout)?

### Takeaway
A full-titanium single-exit cat-back: Ø80 mm piping in three slip-jointed sections, one round, polished-titanium silencer (Ø152 mm) and **one large round Ø115 mm straight-cut tip** with a heat-blued (burnt blue/purple) titanium outlet and laser-etched "EXPREME Ti" logo, exiting under the rear bumper in the factory single-exit position. It weighs 4.85 kg against the stock 16 kg system.

### Cited Findings
- Spec table: titanium; main pipe Ø80.0 × 1 mm; silencer Ø152 × 1 mm; exit Ø115 × 1 mm; flange 6.0 mm; 4.85 kg (stock: iron, Ø60.5 pipe, 100 × 440 mm oval silencer, Ø100 exit, 16.00 kg). — [Tomei catalogue: ti-evo7-9](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/ti-evo7-9/)
- Layout: "racing layout" with as few bends as possible; three-section pipe assembly joined with springs and clamp bands instead of flanges; "compatible with all CT9A Evo models (Japanese bumper)"; the page shows "Style" photos on an Evo 8 and an Evo 9. — same source
- Kit contents include main pipe A, main pipe B, silencer assembly, a SOUND REDUCER (removable baffle) with button bolt and Allen key, muffler hangers, spacers, and an A/F boss blind plug. — same source; a US retailer lists "1 - Titanium Exhaust Tip, 1 - Sound Reducer V2" for the TB6090 kit. — [MAPerformance (search snippet)](https://www.maperformance.com/products/tomei-expreme-ti-cat-back-exhaust-2003-2006-mitsubishi-evolution-8-9-tb6090-mt01b)
- RallySport Direct titles the US version "Tomei Expreme Ti Full Titanium Catback Exhaust - Burnt Tip - 2003-2006 Mitsubishi Evo 8/9". — [RallySport Direct](https://www.rallysportdirect.com/part/cat-back-exhaust-system/tb6090-mt01b-tomei-expreme-ti-titanium-catback-exhaust)
- Visual check of Tomei's official photos (observed directly): single straight-cut round tip, mirror/brushed raw titanium can, blue-to-purple heat colouring on the last few cm of the tip and a burnt band at the rear of the canister, "EXPREME Ti" etched on the tip; on the white Evo 9 photo the tip sits under the bumper near the car's right-rear corner (the factory exit side). — [Ti_CT9A_set.jpg](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/search/img/searchimage/open/Ti_CT9A_set.jpg); [9_oki.jpg](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/9_oki.jpg); [evo789_back.jpg](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/evo789_back.jpg)
- Noise: Tomei publishes no dB figure for this muffler; it is marked "FOR OFF ROAD USE ONLY" (JP: not usable on public roads). Tomei's dyno chart compares stock vs Tomei muffler vs muffler + Ti cat straight pipe on a CT9A but without numeric scale values visible in the image. — [Tomei catalogue: ti-evo7-9](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/ti-evo7-9/); [graph_ti_789.jpg](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/graph_ti_789.jpg)
- US street price of the TB6090 versions was about US$1,090–1,290 across retailers. — [Import Image Racing](https://www.importimageracing.com/products/tb6090-mt01b-tomei-expreme-ti-titanium-cat-back-exhaust-mitsubishi-evo-8-evo-9-2003-2006-tb6090-mt01b); [Bulletproof Automotive](https://bulletproofautomotive.com/?p=361193)

### Inferences
- For the photo configurator, the Tomei option is a single Ø115 mm tip in the stock position: the closest existing layer is a "single tip, burnt titanium" variant, scaled up from the stock Ø100 mm tip. It is not a twin, quad or centre layout.
- Singapore-market Evo IXs (and most grey imports) are RHD Japan/UK-type cars, so the Japanese-bumper version (440003 / TB6090-MT01A) is the relevant one; confirm the rear bumper with the client.

### Gaps
- No official tip-shape term (e.g. "straight cut" vs "slash") or tip-length figure; description of the tip is from the photos.
- No published dB value; no JASMA certification.
- The "renewed" replacement model Tomei announces (リニューアル) was not found on the site yet; its look is unknown.

## Which are current vs discontinued?

### Takeaway
Current with stock (Nov 2026): Ti cat straight pipe, heat protector, ARMS M7963, BOV, suction hose, Poncam/Poncam Type-R EVO9, most Procams, valve springs, Ti retainers, cam gear, head gaskets, timing/balancer belts, pistons, rods, bearings, main studs, thermostat killer, fuel pumps, oil caps. ## What power figures does Tomei quote for ARMS turbos on the 4G63?

### Takeaway
Tomei quotes **360–370 PS** for the ARMS M7963 on a stock-bottom-end 4G63 (limited by the factory conrods, at 1.4–1.5 kgf/cm² / 19.9–21.3 psi with a boost controller) and **420–430 PS** on a 2.2 L or 2.3 L stroked engine (1.6 kgf/cm² / 22.8 psi). The older M7960 guide gave 360–370 PS and 400–420 PS. US retailers rate the US-market kits at about 400 hp (MX/BX7960) and 430 hp (MX/BX7967).

### Cited Findings
- M7963 target power: approx. 360–370 PS (stock engine; "maximum of 360–370 ps is recommended" because of conrod strength) and approx. 420–430 PS (2.2/2.3 L tuned engine). Boost: 1.4–1.5 kgf/cm² stock engine, 1.6 kgf/cm² tuned, set in 4th/5th gear with a boost controller; no actuator adjustment needed. — [Tomei catalogue: arms-4g63](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/catalogue/arms-4g63/)
- M7963 recommended supporting parts: Poncam Type-R; Tomei metal head gasket; injectors 620 cc+ (stock engine) / 760 cc+ (2.2/2.3 L); fuel pump 165 L/h+ / 220 L/h+ at 3 kg/cm² (Tomei 255 L/h recommended); adjustable FPR (Tomei Type-S) set to 3 kg/cm²; high-flow air filter; Expreme manifold and outlet/front pipe; high-flow metal cat; muffler with Ø80 main pipe (Expreme Ti); Tomei suction hose. — same source
- M7963 is designed for "smoothness and powerfulness especially at high RPM" with response "equal to stock trim"; stock power falls away from about 6,000 rpm while the M7963 holds it to the top. — same source; [M7963 manual](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/manuals_data/pdf/arms_4g63_m7963.pdf)
- Older ARMS M7960 setup guide: "capable of 400PS with 1.6k of boost"; expected 360–370 PS stock engine, 400–420 PS on 2.2/2.3 L; injectors 620 cc+ (Tomei 700 cc recommended); spark plugs heat range 8–9. — [Tomei M7960 setup guide](https://www.tomei-p.co.jp/_2003web-catalogue/000_arms/product_arms_m7960_guide_e.html)
- US-market kits: BX7960 ~400 hp and BX7967 ~430 hp (ball bearing); MX7960 "400HP-capable" and MX7967 430 hp (journal bearing, 0.77 A/R, initial boost 1.0 kgf/cm²). — [Kure Motorsport MX7967](https://www.kuremotorsport.com/products/tomei-arms-mx7967-j-b-turbo-kit-for-mitsubishi-lancer-evolution-evo-4-to-9-4g63); [Kure Motorsport BX7967](https://www.kuremotorsport.com/collections/tomei-usa/products/tomei-arms-bx7967-b-b-turbo-kit-for-mitsubishi-lancer-evolution-evo-4-to-9-4g63); [Titan Motorsports MX7960](https://titanmotorsports.com/products/j-b-turbocharger-kit-arms-mx7960-4g63-evo4-9)

### Inferences
- For marketing copy in Singapore, "up to 360–370 PS on a stock engine, 420–430 PS with a Tomei 2.2/2.3 L stroker" are Tomei's own numbers; the higher US hp figures are retailer quotes for different (older) kits.

### Gaps
- No Tomei dyno sheet with exact Evo IX MIVEC figures was found; Tomei's figures are generic 4G63 (Evo IV–IX).
- The US kits' hp figures could not be checked on Tomei USA's own site.
