# HKS exhaust, suspension and chassis/visible parts for the Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A)

Method note: almost everything below comes straight from HKS's own application tables and product-database pages (hks-power.co.jp, EN and JP), cross-checked against HKS USA (hksusa.com) and HKS's archived USDM price lists. JPY prices are HKS Japan retail, shown "incl. 10% tax (ex-tax)". The descriptions of how parts look come from my own viewing of the official HKS photos linked as sources. HKS tables mark "Close Sound Level" (近接排気音, the Japanese proximity test) and "Idling Sound Level" separately. Both are in dB, stock vs HKS.

## Q1. Which HKS exhaust models exist for the CT9A Evo IX, with part numbers, and what do the tips look like?

### Takeaway
HKS Japan lists exactly three JDM cat-back mufflers for the Evo IX / IX MR: the **Super Turbo Muffler 31029-AM001**, the **silent Hi-Power 31019-AM008** and the **Hi-Power SPEC-R 31030-AM001** (titanium, make-to-order). There are also four US-market systems (Hi-Power 31006-BM001/BM002, LEGAL 31013-BM001, Carbon-Ti 31012-BM001) and a now-gone US "Racing Muffler" 31701-BM002. Every one has a **single round tip at the stock exit** (right rear). None is twin, quad, centre or slash-cut. HKS makes **no Legamax (Sports/Premium), Hi-Power SPEC-L/L II, Super Dragger, Cool Style or Super Turbo Muffler Ti for the CT9A**; those exist only for other cars, including the CZ4A Evo X. The pipes ahead of the muffler are the Metal Catalyzer 33005-AM002 and the GT Extension Kit 14018-AM001 (turbo-outlet extension plus front pipe).

### Inferences
- For the configurator, the honest HKS exhaust choices for an Evo IX are:
  - the Super Turbo Muffler: a blue-tinted 124 mm tip with slots, the most distinctive look
  - the silent Hi-Power: plain polished 120 mm
  - the Hi-Power SPEC-R: titanium 120 mm, dark/blue
  - optionally the US-market Carbon-Ti (carbon silencer, burnt tip) and LEGAL (black body, polished 106 mm tip)

  All are single-exit at the stock right-rear position. The site's twin, quad and centre exhaust layouts do not match any HKS Evo IX product.
- 31030-AM001 appears to have replaced the older SPEC-R codes 31025-AM003 and 31006-AM003: HKS USA uses the 31025-AM003 photo for 31030-AM001, and 31006-AM003's data is garbled. This is inference.
- The US BM-suffix systems were made for the USDM Evo VIII/IX (2003–2006). Singapore cars are mostly JDM/RHD, so the JDM codes (AM-suffix) are the natural catalogue choices.

### Gaps
- No official Evo-specific photo or tip description was found for the US Hi-Power 31006-BM001/BM002. Whether its tip is polished or titanium-coloured is unconfirmed: the HKS DB pages have no image, and hksusa.com shows only a logo.
- No official studio photo of the CT9A SPEC-R silencer is confirmed. The series-page studio shot is not labelled by car.
- HKS publishes no noise figures for the BM-suffix US systems, and no product weights for any Evo IX muffler.
- How current the US-market items are is unclear. hksusa.com flags them "not discontinued" (except 31701-BM002, which has no page), but HKS Japan marks 31006-BM001 "To be discontinued". I could not confirm stock.

## Q2. Which HKS Hipermax (coilover) models exist for the CT9A, with part numbers?

### Takeaway
The current HKS CT9A coilovers are the **HIPERMAX S 80330-AM002** (rubber upper mounts), **HIPERMAX S 80330-AM002P** (front pillow-ball upper) and **HIPERMAX R 80310-AM002**. All three fit every CT9A (VII, VIII, VIII MR, IX, IX MR, and the Wagon for the S). The discontinued CT9A models are the previous **HIPERMAX S 80300-AM002P**, **MAX IV GT 80230-AM002** and **MAX IV SP 80250-AM002**. No HIPERMAX IV GT, D' or MAX IV Street-type kit for the CT9A appears in HKS's current table.
