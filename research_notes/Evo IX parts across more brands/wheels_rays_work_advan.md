# Rays (Volk Racing, Gram Lights), Work and Advan Racing (Yokohama) wheels for the Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A)

Scope and method (read first):
- Pages read 2026-11-26. Most data comes from the makers' own Japanese sites:
  - Rays: rayswheels.co.jp product pages `/products/brand/detail/{id}`. Their size tables give price, inset, face and a caliper-clearance column.
  - Work: work-wheels.co.jp product pages, plus Work's own "search by car" matching for the CT9A.
  - Yokohama Wheel: yokohamawheel.jp `brand/…/index_jpn.html` size tables.
- **Prices** are Japanese list prices per wheel, including 10% consumption tax, as printed by the maker. They are for internal reference only.
- **Rays caliper column:** each size row carries symbols for example cars whose big brakes it clears. On every Rays page read, `☆` means "LANCER EVO.(VII～IX, X)", `∑` means "LANCER EVO.(VII～IX)" and `★` means "LANCER EVO.X" only. The rows print `∑` as a Greek `Σ`. Rays adds that these are reference examples and the size should be chosen with the dealer. Below, **[E]** marks a size that Rays tags `☆` or `Σ`, i.e. one it lists as clearing Evo VII–IX calipers.
- **Could not reach** (blocked by the session network, or HTTP 403): mackinindustries.com (Rays USA), workwheelsusa.com, vividracing.com, apexwheels.com and driftworks.com. Retailer data from those sites comes only from search-result titles, and is marked as such. Weights were not published on any maker page read.
- **Work price/size PDFs** (catalogue pages) render without their table text, so Work's full size and price lists could not be read. Work's sizes below come from its CT9A matching tool plus retailer listings.
- **Images** were checked with `curl -sIL` (HTTP 200 and an image content type). A copy of each was viewed in the session scratchpad to describe the design; none was saved to the repo.

## Catalogue: one table per brand (summary of all key questions)

### Takeaway
There are 19 models or editions to consider. All but the Work CR Kiwami, the GT Premium Version, the RS-II 18-inch, the original Advan RS and the Work S1R are current. Each is made in 5x114.3 at 18x9.5 within or near +22 to +45 (the S1 3P can be built to any inset).

The only maker-confirmed **Evo VII–IX caliper clearance** at 18x9.5 is from Rays. It covers the TE37 SAGA SL +22, CE28N-plus +45/+22/+12, CE28N-plus SL +44/+39/+21/+11 and 57DR SPEC D +22/+12.

Work's own CT9A matching lists Meister S1 3P 18x8.5 +37, CR Kiwami 18x8.5 +30 and CR Shigoku 18x8.5 +30. Advan publishes no car-specific caliper data, but says the RG-D2's 18-inch rim profile takes rotors up to 400 mm, depending on the caliper.

### Inferences
- For the configurator's "popular aggressive 18x9.5" slot, the best-documented picks are:
  - **TE37 SAGA SL 18x9.5 +22** (Rays tags it for Evo VII–IX calipers)
  - **CE28N-plus 18x9.5 +22 or +45**, or the SL version
  - **57DR SPEC D 18x9.5 +22**
  - **Advan RG-D2 18x9.5 +22 (Super GTR face)**
  - **Advan GT BEYOND 18x9.5 +29/+38**, not maker-confirmed for the Evo
- For a stock-looking width, use 18x8.5 +30 to +37: the Work models (Work's matching) and Rays CE28N/57DR [E] sizes.
- Visually the list falls into a few distinct "families", which makes it simple to render:
  - six-spoke: TE37 family, 57DR, RG-D2
  - ten-spoke: CE28N-plus, ZE40 (five pairs), RS-II/RS-III/RS
  - twin five-spoke: CR Kiwami, CR Shigoku
  - five-spoke: GT family, Meister S1 3P

## Sizes and offsets in 5x114.3 that suit the Evo IX, caliper clearance, construction and weight

### Takeaway
Every model on the list is made in 5x114.3 at 18 inches, and most also at 17. The 18x9.5 sizes cluster at +22, +29/+30, +35/+38 and +45.

Rays is the only maker that marks sizes for Lancer Evo VII–IX brakes, and it does so selectively:
- Among 18x9.5 sizes, it tags the TE37 SAGA SL +22, CE28N-plus +45/+22/+12, CE28N-plus SL +44/+39/+21/+11 and 57DR SPEC D +22/+12.
- It does **not** tag the standard TE37 SAGA S-plus at 18x9.5. Its +22 and +38 carry only the Evo X symbol.
- It tags no ZE40 size for the Evo.

Work's CT9A matching suggests 18x8.5 +30 to +37, with notes on fender contact. Advan states only a 400 mm rotor allowance for the RG-D2 and caliper-driven concave profiles for the GT BEYOND.

### Inferences
- Rays' Evo tags are sparse and look conservative. The TE37 SAGA S-plus has no Evo VII–IX tag even at 18x9.5 +22, while the TE37 SAGA SL at the same width and offset is tagged.
  - The SL's own text says "装着が出来ない車種グレードがあります" (some car grades can't fit). The difference is probably the spoke back profile and the face number (both F4 here).
  - For the configurator, treat the [E] sizes as "maker-listed for the Evo" and the rest as "fit to be confirmed by the workshop".
- The Advan RG-D2's 400 mm rotor allowance is well above the Evo's 320 mm front disc. The 18-inch RG-D2 Super GTR sizes (+22 to +35) are therefore likely to clear the stock Brembo, but Yokohama does not say so for this car.
- The Evo IX hub bore is commonly quoted as 67.1 mm, against 73.1 mm (Rays) and 73 mm (Advan), so hub rings would be expected. The 67.1 mm figure was not verified in this session.
