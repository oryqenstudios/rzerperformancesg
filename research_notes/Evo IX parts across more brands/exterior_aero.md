Research date: 26 Nov 2026. Scope: carbon bonnets (Varis, Seibon, Chargespeed, Rexpeed, plus Voltex), GT wings (Voltex, APR GTC-300, plus the Varis wing), full kits and lips (Varis, Chargespeed, Do-Luck, plus Voltex and APR), canards, rear diffusers and front tow hooks, for a right-hand-drive JDM/UK-spec Evo IX (2005–2007) in Singapore.

## For each model: exact Evo IX fitment, part numbers, material, finish options, design in words, replace-or-add, and status

### Takeaway
Varis is the deepest Evo IX-specific range and the best documented. Its own 2026 price list gives:
- two cooling bonnets (VBMI-114/115 "EVO9 Version 2", VBMI-110/111 "EVO9 S耐 Version")
- the '09 Ver.2 front bumper, rear diffuser Ver.2, side skirts and canards
- the GT-Wing Euro Edition with an Evo 8/9 bolt-on bracket (VGW-M02F)

Several Varis carbon variants were discontinued in **February 2026**.

The other brands:
- **Seibon:** sells wet-carbon Evo 8/9 bonnets: OE, OE-DRY, CW, CWII and TSII (HD0305MITEVO8-xx).
- **Voltex:** the CT9A Type-5 GT wing is 1600 mm wet carbon on a "special mount" that uses the factory spoiler holes. Voltex also makes the Cyber bumpers, the WTAC dry-carbon bonnet and diffusers.
- **APR GTC-300:** 61″ (AS-106148) or 67″ (AS-106748) on black billet-aluminium pedestals.
- **Do-Luck:** makes a full CT9A kit (front bumper with bonnet top moulding, side diffusers, rear bumper, blister fenders, canards, aero trunk). All of it is made to order.
- **Rexpeed (Taiwan):** makes Evo 9 bolt-on carbon pieces (canards, splitters, a JDM-bumper diffuser, a bonnet vent) but **no bonnet**.
- **Chargespeed:** only a "Bottom Line" front lip and a Type-2 front bumper could be confirmed for the CT9A. No Chargespeed CT9A bonnet was found.
- **Tow hooks:** no Beatrush or Cusco front hook for the CT9A was found. Move Over Racing sells a red or matte-black aluminium Evo 8/9 front hook.

## LTA rules for body kits, wings, canards, bonnets and tow hooks in Singapore

### Takeaway
LTA's OneMotoring modification page (read in full; "Last updated 06 August 2026") sets the rules for these parts:
- **Spoilers, bumpers and side skirts** need no approval if they meet conditions: no protrusion from the car body, no sharp corners or edges, and no blocking of the driver's view. Bumpers must also be of a design "approved by the vehicle manufacturer", and skirts must never touch the road.
- **Tow hooks** are on the official **"not allowed"** list.
- **Bonnets, canards, diffusers and widebody fenders** are not named on the page. A secondary source says a carbon bonnet needs supporting documents (see the parallel note).
- **Penalties:** individuals up to S$20,000 and/or two years in jail (doubled for repeat offenders); workshops up to S$40,000 (S$80,000 for repeat offenders).

### Cited Findings
All of the official text below is from the OneMotoring page, read directly: [LTA OneMotoring, Vehicle Technologies and Modifications](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/owning/modification.html).

- **Spoilers:** "These requirements must be met when installing spoilers: They should not protrude from the car body laterally or longitudinally. They should not have any sharp corners and edges. They should not obstruct the driver's view." Listed under "Modifications that do not need LTA's approval".
- **Bumpers:** "These requirements must be met when changing or modifying bumpers: The bumper design is approved by the vehicle manufacturer. The bumper must not have any sharp corners or edges." No approval needed.
- **Side skirts:** "They should not protrude from the car body laterally. They should not have sharp corners and edges. The lowest point of the side skirts should not come into contact with the road surface at any time." No approval needed.
- **Suspension (relevant to lowered aero cars):** "The lowest point of the vehicle body should not come into contact with the road surface at any time. No part of the vehicle bodywork should be modified to accommodate the suspension system."
- **Sports rims and tyres:** rims and tyres "should not protrude from the car body laterally" / "must not protrude from the vehicle body laterally". The tyre rule also says "The overall rolling radius of the wheels and tyres should remain the same as the original". This matters for widebody kits with wider wheels.
- **Tow hooks are banned:** under "Modifications that are not allowed": "Tow hooks: Tow hooks have sharp and hard surfaces that may aggravate injuries in the event of a collision."
- **Other banned items on the page:** air horns, chassis modification ("The chassis is an integral part of a vehicle and it cannot be modified"), crash bars, aftermarket DRLs, decorative lamps, engine-capacity increases, aftermarket HID kits, nitrous, uncovered spot lamps, and tinting or masking of lamps.
- **Who fits parts:** "All modifications must be installed by qualified personnel according to the vehicle and product manufacturer's recommendations."
- **Penalties:** "Individuals who illegally modify vehicles face fines of up to $20,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or both. Penalties are doubled for repeat offenders. For non-individuals (e.g. workshops), first-time offenders can be fined up to $40,000 and repeat offenders up to $80,000."
- **What the page does not mention:** bonnets, canards, diffusers, splitters, GT wings (as distinct from "spoilers"), widebody fenders or body width (checked by text search of the full page).
- **Carbon bonnet (secondary):** a non-original-material bonnet needs certification or documents proving it is designed for the vehicle, submitted to LTA's Vehicle Engineering Division. Source: the parallel note `singapore_sourcing_lta.md`, citing [Motorist Q&A 445](https://www.motorist.sg/ask-motorists/445/am-i-allowed-to-modify-my-car-s-bonnet) (not read in this session).
- **Canards (secondary, old):** an LTA reply quoted on a 2010-era forum said protruding objects that endanger road users are not allowed, judged case by case. Source: the parallel note, citing the [SG Evo Club forum](https://www.sgevoclub.com/threads/all-canards-are-illegal.12328/) (not read here; anecdotal).
- **Annual inspection:** a Singapore Evo IX is more than 10 years old and so goes through annual periodic inspection, per the parallel note (`singapore_sourcing_lta.md`, "The Evo IX's age matters").
- **Parts sold as track-only:**
  - Varis labels its Evo IX canard "サーキット専用部品" (circuit-only part) — [Varis JP '09](https://varis.co.jp/product/mitsubishi/mitsubishi-lancer-evolution-%e2%85%b8-%e2%85%b8mr-09ver/)
  - Voltex's WTAC bonnet is "Recommended for competition use" — [Bulletproof](https://bulletproofautomotive.com/product/voltex-wtac-dry-carbon-hood/)
  - Bulletproof's Voltex Type-5 page describes the wing for "downforce and improved stability at high speeds" — [Bulletproof](https://bulletproofautomotive.com/product/voltex-ct9a-wing/)

### Inferences
- **Tow hooks:** these should be shown in the configurator as **track/show only, not road-legal in Singapore**. This is LTA's own wording, not just secondary sources. The same applies to the red hooks seen on the Varis S耐 demo cars.
- **Aftermarket bumpers:** a strict reading of the bumper rule ("design is approved by the vehicle manufacturer") would exclude most aftermarket bumpers: Varis Ver.2, Do-Luck, Voltex Cyber and Chargespeed Type-2. How LTA applies this at inspection was not found. Lips, splitters and diffusers that add to the factory bumper are less exposed, but must still have no sharp edges or protrusion.
- **GT wings:** a GT wing has to stay within the car's outline and have no sharp corners. All three CT9A wings are 1430–1702 mm wide, and the Evo IX is about 1.77 m wide (project CLAUDE.md), so the blade itself sits inside the body width. However, they all sit high on stands, end plates have edges, and a wing whose trailing edge overhangs the rear bumper would "protrude longitudinally". Fitment and edge protectors would decide each case.
- **Widebody kits:** Do-Luck adds about 30 mm per side and APR EVIL-R about 90 mm overall. These are not covered by any rule found. Wheels and tyres must still not stick out past the (new) body. Any chassis cutting is banned.
- **Bonnets:** carbon bonnets should carry a note that LTA documentation may be needed (secondary source). Hood pins or Aerocatch latches, which Seibon recommends, add small protrusions on the bonnet surface.

### Gaps
- **No numeric limits:** no official numeric limits (maximum width, overhang, minimum edge radius) for aero parts were found. The Road Traffic (Motor Vehicles, Construction and Use) Rules on Singapore Statutes Online could not be reached (an earlier note found sso.agc.gov.sg blocked; it was not retried).
- **Bonnets and canards unconfirmed:** no official LTA statement on carbon bonnets or canards was read in this session. Both rest on secondary sources cited in the parallel note.
- **Enforcement practice unknown:** how inspectors treat aftermarket bumpers under the "approved by the vehicle manufacturer" condition is unknown.
