## 1. Exact Evo IX part numbers, variants, specs and status (clutches, rear LSDs, shifter)

### Takeaway
Every brand asked about has current CT9A (Evo VII–IX) parts. All the clutches are **job-card-only** items, and so are the LSDs.
- The stock Evo IX clutch is **pull type**. ATS Spec I, OS Giken STR/R2CD (with their "movement kit") and the Comp1 twin disc convert it to **push**.
- Retailers sell most single-plate kits as "Evo 8/9" or "Evo 7/8/9" without separating 5- and 6-speed. Exedy's Hyper Single VF is the only one explicitly marked "5/6MT".
- Rear LSDs come in two families:
  - **Non-AYC** units (Cusco LSD 141 series, Kaaz SBM2031, OS Giken Super Lock, ATS CMRB8714) fit only the RS or a car already converted to the RS diff.
  - **AYC-housing** units (Cusco LSD 448 series, ATS CMRA/CMRB/CMRD8740, ATS Carbon Hybrid AYC) go into the GSR's AYC diff case.
- **The Torque Solution short shifter TS-SS-012 is 5-speed only**, so it fits an RS but not a 6-speed GSR.

## 2. How the AYC delete is done on an Evo IX, and which LSDs suit it

### Takeaway
There are three ways to delete the AYC on a GSR, and all of them are job card. The ACD (active centre diff) is kept in every case:
1. **Swap to the mechanical RS rear diff.** Ross Sport's kit is the documented example. It bolts onto Evo 5–9 GSRs and usually keeps the GSR propshaft. The two AYC feeds are blanked at the pump and the ACD feed is left alone. Then fit a non-AYC LSD (Cusco 141, Kaaz SBM2031, OS Giken Super Lock, ATS CMRB8714) in the RS case.
2. **Keep the AYC diff case and replace its internals** with an LSD made for the AYC housing: the ATS 8740 family ("AYC disabled, coupler can stay", fits to 2007.9) or Cusco LSD 448 (fitment to the IX disputed).
3. **Disable the AYC and run in fail-safe.** This leaves dash warning lights and is not a proper conversion.

On the controller side, Benchmark Solutions (AU) reprograms the factory **ACD ECU** to an "ACD only (deleted AYC)" map for A$299.

### Inferences
- For a Singapore GSR, the neatest job-card option is likely the in-housing route, **ATS CMR*8740 with the ACD-only tune**. It keeps the GSR axles and diff mounts. The ATS listing implies the AYC coupler and pump stay in place, disabled, which is the same pump-blanking idea Ross Sport describes.
- The RS-diff swap (Ross Sport) is the strongest, most configurable option. It is also the most parts-heavy and probably costs the most once shipping to Singapore is added. That is an inference: Ross Sport showed no price, the product being unavailable at the time.
- Without the ACD-only tune (or an equivalent), AYC fault lights are likely to stay on. The **steering-wheel-and-gauges view** could show this, but a properly converted car looks normal on the dash, so the conversion is best treated as job-card-only.
- Ross Sport describes its RS carrier as from the "Evo 9MR RS". That suggests the JDM Evo IX RS already has the mechanical rear diff, so an RS owner only needs the LSD, not the conversion.

### Gaps
- I could not read a primary source (Mitsubishi service manual, evoscan full page, EvolutionM thread) on:
  - exactly which warning lamps an Evo IX GSR shows with the AYC disabled
  - whether the ACD keeps working in that state without a reprogram
- Whether the Evo IX GSR's rear diff is the same "Super AYC" unit as the Evo VIII is not confirmed here. This matters for whether the Cusco 448 (listed for Evo 7/8) fits the IX.
- Whether the Singapore LTA treats a rear-diff or AYC change as a modification that needs approval was not researched here. The earlier LTA notes say "transmission changes" are reported as needing approval (`singapore_market_and_lta.md`).
- No price was available for the Ross Sport kit (the product was unavailable). I found no Singapore workshop advertising AYC deletes.

## 5. Rough prices and how each brand is sold in Singapore

### Takeaway
**RZ Crew Garage** (rzcrewgarage.com) prices in **SGD with Singapore and Malaysia import taxes included** and ships from Japan. It is the one Singapore-facing source carrying most of these brands for the CT9A: Exedy (Japan range), OS Giken, ATS, Cusco, Kaaz, Competition Clutch and HKS. Blackhawk Japan (SGD) is the other Japan-sourcing channel. **I found no official Singapore distributor** for Exedy, ACT, OS Giken, ATS, Cusco, Kaaz, Competition Clutch or Torque Solution. ACT and Torque Solution showed up only at US retailers.
