## 1. Turbos: which Evo IX models exist, with part numbers, sizes, claimed power, fitment, look in the bay and status?

### Takeaway
For a stock-looking, bolt-on Evo IX turbo, the current options are Forced Performance's colour series (FP54 Green, Green, Red, Black, Zephyr, Zero) in the factory-style MHI turbine housing, plus the FP 71HTA "super core" and the MHI TF06-18K. Kinugawa sells budget billet TD05HR/TD06 hybrids, and GCG (Japan) sells two Garrett G25 reverse-rotation bolt-ons. Garrett makes **no** Evo 8/9 kit of its own: its own kits are for the Evo X. The ATP GTX3076R Gen II Evo 8/9 kits have gone from ATP's site. The bigger FP turbos in FP's stainless housings need an external TiAL MV-S wastegate and a matching downpipe, so they are not stock-location bolt-ons in the full sense.

From the front with the bonnet up, they would look almost the same as stock under the factory heat shield. The visible differences are the compressor cover (FP 84 mm anti-surge port, Kinugawa anti-surge cover), the intake pipe on the compressor inlet and the actuator colour (black on Kinugawa and GCG, gold on Tomei).
- The FP Zephyr and Zero, the stainless-housing FP versions, the ATP Stealth kit and the ETS kits add an **external wastegate** (TiAL MV-S, 44 mm or twin 38 mm) and its dump tube, which would show in an engine-bay close-up.

- Because FP's site was unreachable, the FP four-digit codes (2005xxx) come from retailer SKUs ("FPT 2005xxx"). They agree with each other across MAPerformance and STM, so they are very likely FP's own numbers.
- The US-market Evo IX was sold only as a 2006 model, which is why US retailers list "2006 Evo 9". The JDM/UK Evo IX uses the same 4G63 MIVEC with the same turbo flange, and FP and Kinugawa say Evo IV–IX fit, so these turbos should suit Singapore RHD cars. RHD-specific intake and piping clearances are not stated (see Gaps).

## 6. Singapore legality (LTA) for these parts

### Takeaway
Any turbocharger change needs **LTA approval before fitting**. Penalties have been up to S$20,000 / 2 years' jail for individuals and S$40,000 for workshops since 27 Feb 2026. No LTA source covers intakes or intercoolers either way. Some of these kits (ETS piping) require removing the factory secondary air system on JDM/EDM cars, which is an emissions system and a risk at inspection.

### Cited Findings
- Superchargers or turbochargers need LTA approval, and any turbo change needs prior approval and evaluation. Increasing engine capacity is not allowed. — [AXL.sg](https://axl.sg/guides/car-modifications-singapore-rules); [Torque](https://www.torque.com.sg/advice/modifying-cars-singapore-what-you-can-cannot-do/); [Budget Direct](https://www.budgetdirect.com.sg/car-insurance/articles/car-modifications-impact) (as summarised in the earlier notes, `singapore_market_and_lta.md`)
- Removing catalytic converters and mufflers is not allowed. Exhaust changes need LTA approval with the maker's certificate and lab noise/emission reports. — [LTA OneMotoring](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/owning/modification.html) (search extract, per earlier notes)
- Penalties from 27 Feb 2026: individuals up to S$20,000 and/or 2 years' jail; workshops up to S$40,000; doubled for repeat offenders. — [The Independent SG](https://theindependent.sg/lta-warns-against-illegal-vehicle-mods-as-workshop-fined-and-penalties-raised/) (per earlier notes)
- The earlier research found no source on blow-off valves, intakes or intercoolers. — earlier notes `singapore_market_and_lta.md`
- ETS: "EDM/JDM cars equipped with the factory SAS system requires removal" for its upper intercooler piping. — [ETS piping](https://www.extremeturbosystems.com/products/ets-03-06-mitsubishi-evo-89-2-5-stock-route-upper-intercooler-piping-kit)
- US emissions labels on some of these parts: Mishimoto Evo intercoolers carry CARB EO D-759, and K&N says some intakes "are not legal for sale or use on any pollution controlled motor vehicle in California". — [Mishimoto](https://www.mishimoto.com/mitsubishi-lancer-evolution-7-8-9-race-intercooler-01-07.html); [MAPerformance K&N](https://www.maperformance.com/products/k-and-n-typhoon-intake-system-mitsubishi-evo-8-9-69-6543t-69-6545t)

  - FP stainless housings, Zephyr and Zero: a new downpipe/O2 housing plus an external wastegate whose dump tube is routed into the exhaust.
  - ETS kits: new manifold and downpipe.
  - ATP Stealth: a new 3" downpipe.
  - GCG: fabricated oil, water and intake lines.

### Inferences
- Configurator copy should mark every turbo here as "subject to LTA approval".
- Intakes and intercoolers can be offered without that tag, but without claiming they are approved, since no source covers them.
- Kits that remove the SAS, replace the downpipe or dump wastegate gas need the exhaust rules applied as well, and should not be shown as simple road-legal bolt-ons.
- US CARB numbers have no standing in Singapore. They only show that a part was emissions-tested in the US.

### Gaps
- No LTA source on intakes, intercoolers or SAS removal was found. OneMotoring was unreachable in the earlier session too.
- Whether LTA approval for a turbo needs the turbo maker's documents (FP, Kinugawa and GCG are unlikely to have Singapore paperwork) is unknown.
