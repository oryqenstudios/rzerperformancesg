# Wheels (Enkei, SSR, WedsSport, BBS Japan forged, OZ Racing) and four performance tyres for the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A)

Scope and method (read first):
- Researched 2026-11-26. Almost all figures come from the makers' own current pages and spec sheets, read directly:
  - **Enkei Japan:** the JSON its product pages load sizes and prices from (`/storage/sizePrice.json`).
  - **SSR:** Tanabe USA's ssr-wheels.com spec-list PDFs (US MSRP, weights in lb).
  - **WedsSport:** weds.co.jp spec-table PDFs (2026, JPY incl. 10% tax with the ex-tax price in brackets).
  - **BBS Japan:** bbs-japan.co.jp product spec tables (JPY incl. tax).
  - **OZ Racing:** ozracing.com's **Japanese** pages, which carry size tables naming the cars each size is for, including "MT ランサーEVO" (Mitsubishi Lancer Evo).
  - **Tyres:** y-yokohama.com and yokohamatire.com, tire.bridgestone.co.jp, toyotires.com.
- Prices are per wheel or per tyre, in local currency, for internal reference only. They have not been converted.
- **Blocked or failed:**
  - enkei.com (Enkei USA) returned 403 / no DNS from this session, so US Enkei colour and weight data come only from retailer listings seen in search results.
  - Several SSR pages (GTV02, GTV03, GTX02, SP1R, SP4R) returned a bot-check page.
  - The OZ importer finder is a dynamic map with no readable list.
  - The session's web-search budget ran out near the end. The Singapore distributors for Enkei, OZ, Yokohama and Toyo, and the UTQG figures for the RE-71RS and R888R, could not be searched for. They are listed as gaps.
- **Evo IX baseline** (JDM GSR, GH-CT9A):
  - 235/45R17 93W tyres on 17x8 ET38 wheels, 5x114.3, 67.1 mm centre bore, M12x1.5 thread.
  - Plus sizes 235/40R18 on 18x8 ET38 and 245/40R18 on 18x8.5 ET38.
  - Source: [TireWheelGuide, 2006 Lancer Evolution (CT IX)](https://tirewheelguide.com/sizes/mitsubishi/lancer-evolution/2006/), an aggregator, so treat as secondary.
- **Factory Brembo discs:** 320x32 front and 300x22 rear. Source: [Endless USA kit 1502524](https://endlessusa.com/product/1502524), as recorded in this repo's earlier ENDLESS notes.
- **Text on wheels and tyres:** the configurator draws no text on the car. Each look description below says where the real wheel or tyre carries logos or lettering, so the generated image can leave them out.

## Wheels: which Enkei, SSR, WedsSport, BBS Japan and OZ models fit the Evo IX in 17 and 18 inch (5x114.3), and what are their offsets, construction, weight, finishes, look and status?

### Takeaway
All five brands have current 5x114.3 models in the Evo's sizes.

- **Exact 18x9.5 +38 (the classic aggressive Evo fitment):**
  - Enkei RPF1 (8.3 kg, flow-formed)
  - BBS LM (10.4 kg), RE-V7 (8.9 kg) and RG-R (9.0 kg), all forged
  - WedsSport SA-20R, SA-25R, SA-27R, SA-62R, SA-99R (8.8–9.4 kg) and RN-05M (9.7 kg)
  - SSR GTX03 (21.8 lb / 9.9 kg)
- **Close to it:**
  - WedsSport TC105X 18x9.5 +35 (7.95 kg flow-formed; the Forged is 7.61 kg)
  - Enkei NT03RR 18x9.5 +40
  - SSR GTX04 and GTX05 DR 18x9.5 +40
  - BBS RI-A 18x9.5 +35
- **OZ** is the only maker that names the Lancer Evo in its own size tables, but only at 18x8 and 18x9:
  - Ultraleggera 18x8 +35 and 18x9 +35
  - Leggera HLT 18x8 +32
  - Estrema GT HLT 18x8.5 +35
  - RR40 18x8 +36
  - the track-only Superturismo Magnesio 18x9 +32 (magnesium, 8.43 kg)
- **Discontinued or replaced:** the Enkei NT03+M (replaced by the NT03RR) and the WedsSport TC105N. Neither is in its maker's current line-up.

### Inferences
- **Best fit for the configurator's "classic aggressive" 18x9.5 +38:**
  - RPF1 (exact, the reference look)
  - BBS LM, RE-V7 and RG-R (exact, forged)
  - WedsSport SA-series and RN-05M (exact, cheapest)
  - SSR GTX03 (exact)
- **Close, stiffer alternatives:** the TC105X / TC105X Forged at +35, and the GTX04 / GTX05 DR / NT03RR at +40. The 3 mm differences won't show in a photo.
- **OZ** offers no 9.5J for the Evo. Its Evo-listed sizes are 18x8 to 18x9, which suit a flush, not aggressive, set-up.
- **The Superturismo Magnesio is track-only by OZ's own statement**, so it should not be offered as a street option in Singapore.
- **The RPF1RS's 5x114.3 offsets (+15 or lower)** would poke well out of a standard Evo IX's arches. It is a widebody or flared-arch option only (from comparing with the factory ET38).
- **Brembo clearance:**
  - The only explicit Evo confirmations found are OZ Japan's size lists, which name the Evo.
