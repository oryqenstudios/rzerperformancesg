# Brake upgrades for the Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A, 2005–2007, Brembo GSR): pads, discs and big brake kits

Scope and method (late November 2026):
- Every price and status below was read from the linked page this session, unless marked as a search snippet.
- The Endless Japan and gspeed.com sites could not be reached from this session (network block). Project Mu Japan was reached at `www.project-mu.co.jp` (not `projectmu.co.jp`).
- **ENDLESS:** pads, discs, lines and every CT9A caliper kit are already covered in `research_notes/Tomei and HKS Evo IX parts/endless.md`. That file has the part numbers, compounds, temperatures, prices, colours and verified photos. Only a summary and the cross-references are repeated here.
- **HKS Brake System:** an ENDLESS-built 6POT/Racing6 + Racing4 kit, covered in `research_notes/Tomei and HKS Evo IX parts/hks_exhaust_chassis.md` (Q5).
- **Singapore market and LTA:** also covered in `research_notes/Tomei and HKS Evo IX parts/singapore_market_and_lta.md` (section 6).

**The car these parts fit:** the Evo IX GSR's factory Brembo set-up.
- Discs: 320×32 mm front, 300×22 mm rear. DBA lists the same sizes for its Evo rotors: front 320×32 mm, minimum 29.8 mm, centre bore 69 mm, 5×114.3; rear 300×22 mm, minimum 20 mm, centre bore 90 mm, 58 mm hat height for the drum-in-hat parking brake.
- Calipers: 4-piston front, 2-piston rear. The front pad shape is shared with the Evo V–VIII Brembo cars and the Subaru GDB/GRB STI Brembo.
- Sources: [Endless USA kit 1502524](https://endlessusa.com/product/1502524), [DBA4418](https://dba.com.au/product/front-4000-series-hd-brake-rotor-dba4418/), [DBA4419S](https://dba.com.au/product/rear-4000-series-t3-brake-rotor-dba4419s/)

## Q1. Brake pads for the factory Brembo calipers: Evo IX part numbers, compounds and temperature ranges (pads don't show on the car)

### Takeaway
All four pad brands make the Evo IX Brembo shapes. Each brand uses one front and one rear backing-plate code, and the compound is a suffix:

| Brand | Front code | Rear code |
|---|---|---|
| Endless | EP357 | EP291 |
| Project Mu | F506 | R906 (Subaru-numbered, same shape) |
| Ferodo Racing | FCP1334 | FCP1562 |
| Dixcel | 341225 | 325499 |

Ferodo compound suffixes are H (DS2500) and W (DS1.11).

Street/track-day choices: Endless MX72, Project Mu HC+ / HC-M1 / B-Spec, Ferodo DS2500, Dixcel Z/S/ES. Ferodo DS1.11, Project Mu Club Racer / Racing, and Dixcel RA/RE/R01 are circuit pads.
- There is no Dixcel "REX" in Dixcel's current line-up.
- Project Mu's Japanese site lists "HC M1", HC+ and HC-CS18 as current.

### Inferences
- A pad option in the configurator changes nothing on the photo, so it should "pulse the job card" as the site already does for hidden parts.
- A sensible street-to-track ladder for the Evo IX, by rotor temperature ceiling:
  - Dixcel ES 600 °C
  - Project Mu B-Spec 500 °C
  - Endless MX72 700 °C
  - Dixcel S 700 °C
  - Project Mu HC+ 800 °C
  - Dixcel Z 850 °C
  - Ferodo DS2500 500 °C (track day; no ECE R90)
  - Ferodo DS1.11, Project Mu Club Racer, Dixcel RA (circuit; not for the road)
- Ferodo's DS2500 upper figure (500 °C) is lower than Endless's or Project Mu's road-track pads on paper. Each brand measures differently, so don't rank across brands on this number alone.

### Inferences
- **Lowest risk** to show as street upgrades: same-size pads, discs and lines on the stock Brembo calipers. They change no mounting points and no wheel size.
- **Big brake kits:**

  - Treat 355–380 mm kits as "subject to LTA/inspection advice" in the configurator copy.
  - The 17-inch-compatible kits (AP CP5555-1032, CP5108-1003; ENDLESS brake kits on the 320 mm disc) avoid the rim question.
- **Pad copy:** avoid suggesting road use for circuit-only compounds (Ferodo DS1.11, Project Mu Club Racer/Racing, Dixcel RA/RE/R01).
- **Distributors:** Braking Point (AP + Ferodo) and Maximus Racing (Alcon) are official channels if the client wants to source locally. Project Mu has two official distributors. DBA and Girodisc would be self-imported.
