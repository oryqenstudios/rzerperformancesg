## Business facts (public)

- Two related companies share the address at Carros Centre, 60 Jalan Lam Huat, Singapore: **RZER Pte Ltd** (incorporated 2019; repair and maintenance of motor vehicles incl. installation of parts and accessories, plus retail of parts) and **RZER Performance Pte Ltd** (incorporated 2020). Sources: `https://rzer.sg/home/`, `https://www.sgpbusiness.com/company/Rzer-Performance-Pte-Ltd`, `https://recordowl.com/company/rzer-pte-ltd`.
- Facebook describes RZER Performance as "Vehicle Workshop. Car Modifications and Accessories." Source: `https://www.facebook.com/RZERPerformance.SG/`.
- Linked to **Team RZER**, a grassroots motorsport / time-attack team started in 2011. Source: `https://rzer.sg/teamrzer/`.

## Car makes

- **Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution IX (CT9A)**: the team's road-registered "RZEREVO" V1 and V2 time-attack cars; the shop's social posts claim V1 holds the Sepang semi-slick record (2:10.968; V2 2:11.716; self-reported). Sources: `https://www.instagram.com/rzer.sg/` (search snippet), `https://rzer.sg/teamrzer/`.
- **Toyota Supra**: a team car. Source: `https://rzer.sg/teamrzer/`.
- **Nissan 370Z**: fastest overall in RWD at TarmacAttack 2023 Round 1. Source: `https://rzer.sg/home/` / `https://rzer.sg/tarmacattack/`.
- ENDLESS pad listings cover fitments for VW Golf Mk7 R, Audi S3 / quattro models, MINI Cooper (a "MINI" product tag), Honda Civic Type R FD2R, Toyota GR Supra (DB42) / BMW Z4. *(These are catalogue fitments, not proof of cars worked on.)*

*(Inference: the focus looks like Japanese performance cars, the Evo above all, with brakes for European hatches too.)*

## Not seen in any public source

- No source showed TOMEI, and HKS showed only as one boost controller. The site's current 81-part catalogue (HKS, TOMEI, ENDLESS) is the client's own pick (see `research_notes/Tomei and HKS Evo IX parts/`), not something these public sources show RZER stocking, apart from ENDLESS.
