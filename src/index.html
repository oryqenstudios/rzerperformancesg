Built around how you drive

Rzer tunes and modifies road cars for owners who want them sharper, faster and unmistakably theirs. Every build starts with how the car is used day to day, then gets planned part by part before anything is unbolted.

What goes on the lift

  • ECU remaps

    Stage 1 and Stage 2 software, matched to your hardware and fuel.

  • Intake and exhaust

    Downpipes, cat-backs and valved systems, fitted and mapped together.

  • Suspension

    Coilovers, lowering springs and alignment set up for Singapore roads.

  • Aero and body

    Lips, skirts, diffusers, wings and widebody kits, test-fitted before paint.

  • Wheels and brakes

    Fitment checked for offset and clearance. Big-brake kits and caliper colours.

  • Wraps and carbon

    Gloss, satin and colour-change film, carbon trim and window tint.

See what changes under the skin

Power, chassis, brakes and exhaust are planned as one system. Upgrade the turbo and the brakes, cooling and suspension have to keep up, so we spec them together.

How a build runs

  1. 01

    Consult

    Tell us the car, how you use it and what you want from it.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We spec parts that work together and quote before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Fit

    Parts go on in our workshop, test-fitted, torqued and aligned.

  4. 04

    Tune

    Software is mapped to the new hardware and checked on the road.

  5. 05

    Hand-over

    You collect the car with a job sheet listing everything that was done.
