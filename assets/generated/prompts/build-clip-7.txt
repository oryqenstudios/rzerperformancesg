One continuous shot ending exactly on the last frame. The cabin is already formed and stays exactly as it is, with its doors shut. Glossy black liquid flows forward from the base of the windscreen and up from the floor over the engine and the front wheels and sets into the front of the car: the front wings close over the wheels, the bonnet covers the engine, and the front bumper, grille and headlight housings form over the intercooler, until the complete car of the last frame stands there in glossy black liquid with its headlights off. Nothing opens. The camera stays head-on and centred. The workshop lights, the hexagon ceiling and the red neon stay steady, reflected in the glossy black floor. Photorealistic, cinematic, smooth motion, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
