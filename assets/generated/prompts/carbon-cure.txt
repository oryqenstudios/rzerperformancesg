Locked-off camera, head-on to the parked car, centred in the frame; the camera does not move or turn, the framing stays the same from the first frame to the last. The car does not move. At the start its whole body is glossy black liquid and the headlights are off. After half a second the headlights snap on, a quick flicker and then full brightness. Then a thin bright line of light sweeps over the body from the front bumper back over the bonnet and roof, and behind it the black liquid sets into exposed carbon fibre weave, panel by panel, until the car is the carbon Evolution IX of the last frame. The number plate melts away into the bumper as the carbon sets. The workshop lights, the hexagon ceiling and the red neon stay steady the whole time, reflected in the glossy floor. Photorealistic, cinematic, smooth. No people, no text, no other cars, no scene cut.
