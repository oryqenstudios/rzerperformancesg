One continuous shot ending exactly on the last frame. The glossy black dome slowly sinks and shrinks into a small flat drop with a tiny peak on top. Then it suddenly bursts: thin straight black rods shoot out flat across the floor in every direction like a starburst, quickly multiplying into hundreds that reach far across the floor, while a thin spike rises from the centre. The camera rises and tilts down as the rods spread. The workshop lights, the hexagon ceiling and the red neon stay steady, reflected in the glossy black floor. Photorealistic, cinematic, smooth motion, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
