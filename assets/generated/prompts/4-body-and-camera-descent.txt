One continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts. The wet glossy jet-black liquid bodywork slowly melts and drains down off the car like ferrofluid, revealing underneath a glossy black chassis frame with the engine, a front-mount intercooler, coilover suspension, two seats, the steering wheel and the four wheels. At the same time the camera slowly cranes up and tilts down, rising high above the front of the car until it looks steeply down onto the bare chassis from above the front bumper. Same dark underground car park, same single overhead spotlight and pool of light on the polished concrete floor. Photoreal, realistic liquid physics, no people, no text.
