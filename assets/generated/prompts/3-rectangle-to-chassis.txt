One continuous shot, no cuts. The engine, seats, wheels and suspension turn into glossy black liquid that spreads flat across the concrete floor. The liquid forms a flat rectangular outline on the floor, with dozens of thin straight glossy black rods lying flat on the floor and radiating from the centre out to the rectangle like the spokes of a wheel, linked by fine web-like lines. Then the rods slide inward along the floor and gather at the centre into one small round glossy black drop sitting on the floor. The camera slowly descends and moves closer to the small drop. Same dark underground car park and single overhead spotlight on the polished concrete. Photoreal, realistic liquid, no people, no text.
