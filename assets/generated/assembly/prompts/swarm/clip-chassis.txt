A 3D car assembly breakdown, satisfying and precise. The floating engine and its parts finish assembling and lower into place as the car's steel chassis frame drops in from above underneath it; the suspension arms, coilovers, drive shafts and brakes fly to the four corners and lock on, the four wheels fly in from the sides and slot onto the hubs, and the car's bare grey metal body shell builds itself over the chassis piece by piece, floor, sills, pillars and roof rails snapping together, until the half-built car of the last frame stands on its wheels. Every part at its real-world size and proportions. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom, and the workshop, its lit hexagon ceiling, red neon, tool chests, wheel wall and glossy floor stay perfectly still; only the parts move. Photorealistic, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
