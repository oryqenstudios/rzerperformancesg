A 3D car assembly breakdown, satisfying and precise. The car's carbon fibre body panels fly in from all sides and lock onto the bare body shell one after another with precise mechanical movements: the floor and sills, the rear wings, the roof, the four doors swinging in and shutting, the glass, the front wings, the boot lid and rear wing, then the bonnet dropping down and the front bumper snapping on last, until the finished carbon fibre car of the last frame stands complete. Every part at its real-world size and proportions. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom, and the workshop, its lit hexagon ceiling, red neon, tool chests, wheel wall and glossy floor stay perfectly still; only the parts move. Photorealistic, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
