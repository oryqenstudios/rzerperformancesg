A 3D car assembly breakdown, satisfying and precise. The empty workshop; then small car parts start raining in from above and flying in from behind the camera: bolts, washers, bearings, pistons, connecting rods, camshafts, a crankshaft, the turbo. Hundreds of them swirl through the air and converge over the middle of the floor, slotting together in mid-air into the car's four-cylinder engine piece by piece, while control arms, coilover shock absorbers, drive shafts and brake discs with red calipers fly in and hang around it, until the floating exploded arrangement of the last frame. Every part at its real-world size and proportions. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom, and the workshop, its lit hexagon ceiling, red neon, tool chests, wheel wall and glossy floor stay perfectly still; only the parts move. Photorealistic, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
