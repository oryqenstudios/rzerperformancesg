The finished car stands still in the workshop. The camera makes one smooth, continuous move: it swings round in an arc and comes down from the high front corner of the workshop to the low, centred head-on view of the last frame, gliding past the front of the car, which stays parked exactly where it is and does not turn or move. As the camera arrives in front of it, the car's headlights flicker and come on and stay on. The workshop and the car keep their real-world proportions throughout. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
