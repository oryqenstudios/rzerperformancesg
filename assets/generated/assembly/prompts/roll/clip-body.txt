The car's carbon fibre body builds itself onto the rolling chassis like a transforming machine, with precise mechanical movements: panels unfold out of the frame and swing up into place one after another, the floor and sills, the doors folding up and shutting, the front wings, the windscreen pillars and roof rising and locking, then the front bumper and the bonnet swinging down and clamping shut, panel edges locking together with small bursts of sparks, until the complete car of the last frame stands in the pool of light. As the front locks into place, its headlights flicker and come on, and stay on. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom at all, and the dark workshop, the ceiling, the red neon and the floor stay perfectly still; only the car's parts move. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot. No people, no robots, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
