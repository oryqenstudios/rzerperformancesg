A single car wheel rolls on its own across the dark workshop floor: it starts at the back on the left, rolls forward and to the right towards the camera, slows down, wobbles slightly and comes to rest standing upright in the middle of the pool of light, exactly as in the last frame. The camera follows the wheel: it pans right and pulls back smoothly as the wheel rolls, ending on the wide, centred view of the last frame. The wheel keeps its real size, small in the big dark workshop. The workshop, its dim lighting, the unlit hexagon ceiling, the red neon and the tool chests stay unchanged; only the wheel moves, and the camera follows it. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot. No people, no text, no logos, no other objects, no scene cut.
