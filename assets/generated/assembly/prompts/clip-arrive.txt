The empty workshop. One after another the car's parts fly in from out of frame, from above and from both sides, spinning and turning in the air like pieces of a transforming machine: the chassis frame drops in first, then the four wheels roll and spin in from the sides, the engine and turbo swing in from above, then the carbon fibre doors, wings, bonnet and the front of the car. Each part slows down and stops, hovering in its place, until they hang together in the exploded arrangement of the last frame. All lights on the parts stay dark. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom at all, and the workshop, the hexagon LED ceiling, the red neon and the floor stay perfectly still the whole time; only the car parts move. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot. No people, no robots, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
