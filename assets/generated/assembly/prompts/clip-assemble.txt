The hovering parts assemble into the car like a transforming machine, with precise, fast, mechanical movements: the engine drops into the chassis, the wheels swing in and slot onto the hubs, the suspension compresses, the doors and wings rotate and fold into place and shut, the bonnet and the front of the car slide down and clamp shut, panel edges lock together with small sparks and puffs of dust, until the complete car of the last frame stands on the floor. As the front section locks into place, its headlights flicker and come on, and they stay on to the end. Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, turn or zoom at all, and the workshop, the hexagon LED ceiling, the red neon and the floor stay perfectly still the whole time; only the car parts move. Photorealistic, cinematic, one continuous shot. No people, no robots, no text, no logos, no scene cut.
